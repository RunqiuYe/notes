\subsection{Differentiation of monotone functions}

\begin{thm}[Lebesgue's differentiation theorem]
  Let $I \subset \R$ be an interval and let $f : I \to \R$ be a monotone
  function. Then $f$ is differentiable a.e.\ on $I$.
\end{thm}
